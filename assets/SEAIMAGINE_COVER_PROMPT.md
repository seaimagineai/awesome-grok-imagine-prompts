# Original shared-world cover

Created September 24, 2026 with Codex built-in image generation. The initial image was generated without source-image references. A follow-up edit of that generated draft joined the scenes into one space. The notebook, unbranded shoe, tram bridge and paper whale are new editorial imagery, not reused case thumbnails. They illustrate the library's product, cinematic and fantasy categories; they are not tested Grok outputs or three additional prompt recipes.

A continuous ink line and illuminated track link the written prompt concepts to the imagined scenes. This is a visual metaphor, not a product interface or literal sequence of model operations.

The farthest page becomes a FANTASY scene: a small luminous paper whale floating above a miniature island garden growing from the page. Keep each world bold and readable, two or three shapes maximum, no tiny clutter. Arrange worlds as one upward cinematic fan, with enough separation to read all three at a glance; no duplicate subjects or boxes. Rich handcrafted paper edges, convincing physical light, beautiful miniature-photography realism with deliberate magical transformation. Attractive warm coral and honey highlights against deep petrol blue; let the cream notebook be a clean focal anchor.

The words are readable and embedded in the page, connecting written instructions to the unfolding scene; no fake application controls, no fake full prompt, no claim of generated results.
