## Exact generation prompt

```text
Create an original premium SeaImagine brand editorial artwork, wide 16:9 landscape, lavish but disciplined cinematic photographic surrealism. The supplied first reference is the official SeaImagine purple aperture logo, use only its exact visual identity; other references are material/style inspiration, not elements to paste or reproduce as panels. One single continuous sunlit seaside creative atrium, ivory limestone architecture, open ocean horizon, soft lavender shadows, beautiful warm-white morning light. Foreground left a frosted pale-aqua glass perfume bottle on a low integral stone ledge, next to ONE small translucent amber glass pear casting golden caustics, realistically scaled product props. Midground right a refined walnut and brass curved-glass conservatory integrated into the architecture with lush ferns visible inside, not a separate floating diorama. A flowing cobalt-blue pleated textile descends from one ceiling rail and softly trails toward the floor at the right, evoking fashion without a mannequin. One narrow shallow S-curving water channel runs from foreground beside the bottle into the atrium, past the conservatory and out toward the sea; its reflected lavender light provides ONE clear continuous visual route, no arrows or neon loops. Through the far opening, a tiny original paper whale glides above the sea, a subtle fantasy accent rather than a main subject. Strong depth and generous negative space, asymmetric hierarchy, physically shared lighting and perspective, elegant 35mm architectural photography. On the upper left limestone wall, a single tasteful violet aperture emblem faithfully matching supplied official logo and the exact readable word 'SeaImagine' as refined dimensional signage. Sign integrated into real wall, clearly readable at README size, no other text. The brand purple also appears in restrained reflected highlights. No collage, no split panels, no cards, no webpage UI, no buttons, no notebook, no filmstrip, no busy shelf of objects, no repeated logos, no stock watermark. The resulting picture must feel like one desirable imaginative place, with product, architecture, material craft, fashion and fantasy naturally inhabiting it.
```
