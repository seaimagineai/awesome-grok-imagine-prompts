# Social themes behind the original additions

[← Complete prompt index](PROMPT_INDEX.md)

24 newly written English video prompts, in four categories. References below informed subject selection only. We did not copy their prompt wording or media, reproduce their results, or establish a current X trend ranking. These new prompts have not been generation-tested.

The source set combines official YouTube reports about established formats with specific AI creator posts on X. Only the surreal-ASMR report directly identifies an AI video trend. Miniature craft, renovation and fashion reports show broader audience interest; that interest does not prove the new recipes will perform well.

## Original recipe choices

Material examples use a single contact event and close sound; spaces use mechanically defined reveals; miniature scenes place tiny actions inside everyday objects; fashion focuses on fabric, silhouette and controlled movement. These are editorial adaptations of broad formats, not copies of any referenced shot list.

All new briefs use text-to-video, 720p and 5 or 10 seconds. Timing and motion remain creative instructions, not guaranteed model behavior. No reference image is required by these text-to-video prompts.

Source observations, methods and dated metrics are recorded in [the research log](../data/social-category-research.json). Numbers in that log retain their original time windows and are not live popularity rankings.
