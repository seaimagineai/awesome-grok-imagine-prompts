## 1. Pleated Compass — a dancer opening a pleated skirt in one turn

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-1.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 5s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
A full-body portrait of a fictional adult dancer wearing a deep burgundy pleated midi skirt and a fitted ivory long-sleeve top, barefoot on a matte pale-grey studio floor. Her hair is tied in a low bun. Soft side light reveals each fabric ridge without blowing out the top. Frame with comfortable space above her head and below her feet; a plain studio wall fills the background.

For the first second she stands in profile with arms relaxed. During seconds 1–4 she makes one smooth clockwise quarter-turn on the ball of her right foot, letting the skirt fan outward naturally; her left foot lands quietly and her arms open only to shoulder height. In the final second she settles facing the camera while the pleats continue a small delayed sway. Keep the camera locked and the whole body visible.

Sound: a soft foot brush and a short rustle of pleated cloth, with natural studio room tone. No music, applause or dialogue. Preserve the same adult face, bun, outfit and skirt length. Fabric follows rotation and settles under gravity; no costume morph, extra limbs, airborne spin, drifting floor, text or cuts.
```

## 2. Silver Hem Study — a metallic cape crossing a pool of light

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-2.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A wide static stage composition with a single rectangular pool of white light on a charcoal floor. A fictional adult performer in a plain black jumpsuit wears a knee-length silver lamé cape fastened at the shoulders. He enters from the left in three-quarter profile; no audience or scenery is visible. The metallic fabric carries small controlled highlights, retaining real woven texture rather than looking like liquid chrome.

At 0–3 seconds he takes two slow lateral steps into the lit rectangle. At 3–7 seconds he raises both forearms beneath the cape, creating a broad angular silhouette; the hem lifts after his arms, with believable weight and a few uneven folds. At 7–10 seconds he lowers his arms and pauses at the right edge of the light. The cape settles once against the back of his legs.

Sound: measured shoe taps, dry metallic-cloth rustle and quiet stage ambience. No music or speech. Keep his face, black clothing, shoulder fastening and cape length consistent. Maintain a fixed spotlight and grounded feet. No extra performers, mirror doubles, changing fabric colour, sparks, wind machine, text, logos or cuts.
```

## 3. Shadow Collar — a sculptural collar casting a changing silhouette

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-3.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 5s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
An editorial waist-up portrait of a fictional adult woman wearing a matte black jacket with a large asymmetric folded collar, against a warm ivory wall. The collar is a solid piece of stiff fabric, not feathers or separate floating panels. A single hard light from camera-left projects a crisp enlarged shadow to her right. Her short dark hair leaves the collar outline unobstructed; makeup is understated.

In seconds 0–2 she faces slightly left and keeps her shoulders still. From 2–4 seconds she slowly turns her torso about thirty degrees toward camera, causing the collar's angular shadow to narrow across the wall. Her chin follows a moment later. In the final second she stops with a calm neutral expression. Make a very slow push toward her, retaining both the real collar and its whole shadow in the composition.

Sound: a restrained jacket creak and quiet breathing, no words or music. Keep one person, one light and one physically matched shadow. Collar geometry stays intact during the turn. No shadow acting independently, fabric growth, beauty-filter skin, face changes, extra lights, jewellery appearing, text, cuts or flicker.
```

## 4. Ribbon Downbeat — a seated percussionist matching sleeve movement to a beat

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-4.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A medium-wide view of a fictional adult percussionist seated on a low black stool in a terracotta-coloured rehearsal room. She wears a cream tailored suit with two short satin ribbons sewn securely to each sleeve cuff. A single small hand drum rests upright between her knees. Warm soft light from the right reveals satin sheen and suit texture. Both hands, both cuffs and the complete drum remain visible.

During seconds 0–3 she taps a simple alternating left-right rhythm at a relaxed pace; the cuff ribbons follow each wrist with a short delay. During 3–7 seconds the camera moves gently closer as she adds one firm centre strike, then resumes the same two-hand pattern. During 7–10 seconds she ends on one quiet tap, rests both palms on the drumhead and lets all four ribbons settle.

Sound: dry live hand-drum taps with small-room resonance and faint sleeve rustle. No backing track, crowd or voice. Match each audible hit to a visible hand contact. Preserve her adult identity, four attached ribbons, stool and drum. No extra hands, floating accessories, drum deformation, changing costume, logos or cuts.
```

## 5. Velvet Counterstep — two dancers trading a precise sideways step

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-5.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A front-facing full-body shot of two fictional adult dancers on an empty walnut dance floor. The dancer on the left wears a forest-green velvet jacket with black trousers; the dancer on the right wears a rust-coloured velvet jacket with matching dark trousers. Both wear plain low black shoes. A softly lit cream wall and the clean floor provide a simple setting; no mirrors. Keep a visible gap between their bodies throughout.

At 0–3 seconds the left dancer makes one sideways step toward the centre and taps the free toe, while the right dancer holds still. At 3–6 seconds the left returns to the starting mark and the right repeats the same step toward centre. At 6–9 seconds they each take one small outward step and turn their heads toward one another. Hold the final position for the last second. The camera stays fixed; velvet catches light as their shoulders move.

Sound: clearly timed shoe taps and subtle clothing movement, no music or speech. Preserve both adult identities, jacket colours and starting positions. Feet contact the floor without sliding. No crossing bodies, extra partners, merged limbs, acrobatics, wardrobe changes, text or cuts.
```

## 6. Organza Wind Pause — a layered sleeve lifting in a controlled breeze

[TXT](text/en-US/10-fashion-and-performance-6.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 5s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
A close editorial composition from chest to fingertips of a fictional adult model wearing a slate-blue blouse with one large translucent organza sleeve. The model stands side-on against a muted peach studio wall. Her right forearm is held horizontally at waist height, palm down and fingers naturally separated. Broad backlight reveals the layered organza edges, while soft front fill keeps her hand and the opaque blouse body clearly visible.

For seconds 0–1 the sleeve hangs at rest. Between 1–3 seconds a gentle breeze from camera-right lifts its outer layer into a shallow curved arc, exposing the denser inner layer without revealing bare torso. At 3–5 seconds the breeze fades and the outer layer drapes back over the forearm in two soft folds. Make a small lateral camera slide, keeping the hand and sleeve attachment in frame throughout.

Sound: delicate fabric flutter and low room ambience. No music, voice or loud fan. Keep one visible hand with five fingers and a fixed arm position. Both layers remain sewn to the blouse, with consistent translucency and slate-blue colour. No liquid fabric, growing sleeve, floating scraps, skin distortion, text, jewellery or cuts.
```
