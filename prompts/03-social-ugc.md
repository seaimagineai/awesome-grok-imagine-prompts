Choose an offered setting and rewrite the action times; editing, extension and references require a separately supported workflow. See the [actual interface guide](../docs/SEAIMAGINE.md).

The strongest UGC prompts preserve small imperfections: uneven handheld framing, real pauses, natural skin, contact sounds, and ordinary environments. “Authentic” should describe behavior, not lower image quality.

## 1. Shelf Test — honest skincare mini-review

**Mode:** reference-to-video · **References:** `<IMAGE_0>` creator, `<IMAGE_1>` product · **Output:** 10s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
Use the fictional adult creator's identity, face, natural skin, hair, and plain cream T-shirt from
<IMAGE_0>. They hold the exact unbranded tube from <IMAGE_1> in a real bathroom with a slightly
fogged mirror, folded towel, and ordinary shelf. Front-facing smartphone camera, arm's-length,
minor natural framing drift, clear morning window light, no beauty filter.

0–2s: creator raises the tube beside their cheek so its design is visible and says, “Three weeks
in — here's the honest update.” 2–6s: they lower the tube, point once to a natural area of skin,
and say, “Less tight after washing, but I still need moisturizer.” 6–10s: they rotate the tube
once, glance at it, then back to camera: “Good, not magic.” Finish with a small shrug.

Audio: close phone microphone, low extractor fan, one tube-cap click. No music or subtitles.

Continuity: exact identity, face texture, tube design and label, shirt, bathroom layout, and hand
anatomy. No skin transformation, before/after split, invented claims, extra fingers, oversized
product, influencer gestures, new logos, captions, or jump cut.
```

## 2. Twelve-Minute Noodles — one-pan recipe reel

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 12s · 9:16 · 1080p

```text
Opening frame: top-down vertical cooking reel over a well-used carbon-steel pan on a small home
stove. Visible ingredients are fresh noodles, sliced mushrooms, scallions, sesame, and a bowl of
dark sauce. Only one pair of adult hands in rolled denim sleeves. Warm practical kitchen light,
real steam, appetizing but not glossy advertising food, no brands or packaging.

0–3s: hands add mushrooms; they land, sizzle, and begin browning without multiplying. 3–6s: one
hand adds noodles while the other turns them with wooden tongs; noodles bend and stay inside the
pan. 6–9s: sauce pours in one continuous stream and coats the noodles as they are tossed once,
with believable gravity and modest steam. 9–12s: match movement into a close three-quarter view
as scallions and sesame fall; tongs lift one serving and hold it sharply in focus.

Audio: strong synchronized sizzle, wood on metal, sauce pour, quiet kitchen room tone. No speech,
music, timer beep, or captions.

Continuity: same pan, hands, sleeves, utensils, ingredient quantities, and stove. No extra hands,
raw-to-finished teleport, fire flare, food morphing, mess outside pan, logo, or text.
```

## 3. First Set — realistic morning fitness log

**Mode:** image-to-video · **Output:** 9s · 9:16 · 1080p

```text
Preserve the supplied fictional adult athlete, face, body, gray training clothes, squat rack,
barbell load, garage gym, dawn window, and phone-camera composition. Natural body proportions,
visible effort, no physique enhancement.

The athlete takes one breath, braces, and completes exactly one controlled back squat: hips and
knees bend together, heels stay planted, bar remains level across the shoulders, then they stand
to full balance. The camera is fixed on a small tripod with only subtle floor vibration. Hair,
shirt fabric, and breath move naturally; the bar has convincing weight and slight flex. After
standing, the athlete exhales, looks toward the phone, and says, “That moved better than Monday.”

Audio: garage room tone, shoe grip, bar sleeve rattle, controlled inhale and exhale, close natural
voice. No music, cheering, or subtitles.

Continuity: exact identity, body, clothing, rack, plate count, safety bars, grip width, and camera
position. No extra repetition, plate mutation, bending floor, spotter appearing, superhuman speed,
plastic muscles, unsafe bar path, logo, or jump cut.
```

## 4. One Question, One Corner — street interview

**Mode:** reference-to-video · **References:** `<IMAGE_0>` host, `<IMAGE_1>` guest, `<IMAGE_2>` corner · **Output:** 12s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
Use the fictional host from <IMAGE_0> and fictional adult guest from <IMAGE_1> on the quiet city
corner from <IMAGE_2>. Preserve both identities, outfits, microphone, storefront geometry, and
overcast afternoon. Documentary smartphone framing with two people visible from waist up.

0–3s: host turns the microphone toward the guest and asks, “What's one tiny purchase that made
your week better?” 3–9s: guest considers for half a second, smiles, and replies, “A second phone
charger. Boring answer, excellent decision.” 9–12s: host laughs naturally and says, “Honestly,
that's wisdom.” Guest nods once. Maintain eye lines and microphone position between speakers.

Audio: clean close dialogue, light traffic, footsteps, soft wind. Duck ambience during speech.
No music, subtitles, crowd reactions, or brand names.

Continuity: stable faces, height, clothing, microphone, hand count, background, and daylight.
No lip-sync drift, speaker voice swapping, passersby blocking faces, camera cut, beauty filter,
readable shop brands, or added text.
```

## 5. Clay Cup Morning — tactile pottery ASMR

**Mode:** image-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 3:4 · 1080p

```text
Preserve the supplied potter's hands, unfinished clay cup, wheel, apron, wooden tools, wet clay
surface, workshop window light, and close composition. The visual style is precise documentary
macro, not a polished commercial.

0–4s: the wheel turns at a steady moderate speed. Both hands narrow the cup wall with continuous
contact; wet clay moves gradually and retains the cup's overall height. Water glistens and one
small bead travels down to the wheel head. 4–7s: wheel slows; the right hand uses the existing
wooden rib to smooth one outer band while the left supports the inside. 7–10s: wheel stops, hands
release cleanly, and the camera shifts focus from fingertips to the finished rim.

Audio is the subject: damp clay friction, quiet wheel motor, tool scrape, water drop, breathing,
and distant birds. No music, voice, exaggerated squish, or captions.

Continuity: same two hands, cup volume, wheel direction, tool, apron, light, and realistic clay
behavior. No extra fingers, cup inflating, color change, pottery breaking, new tools, logo, or cut.
```

## 6. Umbrella Reset — seamless pet comedy loop

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 6s · 1:1 · 1080p

```text
Opening and ending frame must match for a seamless six-second loop. Square composition in a small
apartment entryway. An original medium-sized shaggy dog sits on a dry mat beside a closed yellow
umbrella in a stand. The dog is slightly damp from rain, looking innocent. Natural phone-video
realism, fixed camera, soft hallway light, no collar tag or branding.

Action: the dog shakes once from head to tail with believable delayed fur motion. Water droplets
fly outward in a modest ring and make the closed umbrella wobble. The dog stops, glances at the
umbrella, then uses one paw to steady it. As the paw returns to the mat, the umbrella and dog
arrive at exactly the opening pose; ambient motion also settles for the loop point.

Audio: one damp fur shake, droplets on tile, umbrella fabric rustle, tiny claw tap. No voice,
laugh track, music, bark, or text.

Continuity: one dog, four legs, same fur markings, closed umbrella, dry-to-slightly-wet mat only,
fixed camera and lighting. No human, umbrella opening, huge splash, cartoon eyes, object morph,
extra tail, logo, or visible loop jump.
```

## UGC direction tips

- Keep spoken copy conversational and short enough to breathe.
- Describe a reason for each glance or gesture; random motion reads as synthetic.
- Lock the phone position, focal length feeling, and background so “handheld” does not become chaos.
- Ask for room tone and one or two recognizable contact sounds instead of a generic music bed.
- Preserve skin texture, pauses, asymmetry, and minor framing drift when authenticity matters.
