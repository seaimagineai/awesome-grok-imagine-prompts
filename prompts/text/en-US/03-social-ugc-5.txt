surface, workshop window light, and close composition. The visual style is precise documentary
macro, not a polished commercial.

wooden rib to smooth one outer band while the left supports the inside. 7–10s: wheel stops, hands
release cleanly, and the camera shifts focus from fingertips to the finished rim.

and distant birds. No music, voice, exaggerated squish, or captions.
