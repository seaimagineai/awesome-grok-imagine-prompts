Opening frame: broad interior of an abandoned botanical glasshouse at blue hour, broken panes,
wet tiled paths, dense ferns, rusted irrigation pipes. A fictional courier in practical gray
clothes runs toward camera carrying one small weatherproof case. Cinematic realism, wide lens,
cool sky light and sparse warm maintenance lamps, no weapons or recognizable branding.

Create one continuous backward tracking shot. The courier runs four strides, plants one foot on
a low stone border, vaults over it with both hands briefly contacting the surface, lands with
weight and two recovery steps, then slides under a fallen irrigation pipe. Leaves react to body
contact, droplets shake from branches, shoes maintain friction, case stays secured against the
torso. Camera retreats on a smooth stabilized path with mild human vibration, never passing
through plants. End as the courier reaches a closed service gate and turns to listen.

Audio: breath, footfalls changing from tile to soil, fabric, case buckle, leaf contact, dripping
water, distant metal echo. No score, gunfire, crowd, or dialogue.

Avoid: impossible flips, weightless movement, limb distortion, duplicated case, broken glass
impact, teleporting camera, aggressive motion blur, or environment redesign.
