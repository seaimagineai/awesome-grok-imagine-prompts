Opening frame: an original sculptural fig made entirely from pale violet glass rests on a charcoal cutting board. Its polished skin encloses tiny amber glass seeds. Extreme side-on macro, the fig fills the lower two-thirds of the vertical frame, a plain dark background and a narrow warm rim light. A steel blade is already touching the top of the fig; only a cropped adult hand holding its handle is visible.

0–2s: the blade presses slowly, making a fine bright contact line. 2–7s: one deliberately surreal, clean cut passes through the solid glass as though through firm jelly; the blade never jumps. 7–10s: a single thin slice tilts away and settles on the board, exposing the seed cross-section. Camera stays locked; shift focus gently from the blade edge to the newly exposed surface.

Audio: a close crystalline scrape followed by one light glass tick as the slice lands. Quiet room, no music or voice. Keep all seeds fixed inside their slices and the remaining fig intact. No shards, exploding fruit, second cut, extra fingers, edible flesh, text or logos.
