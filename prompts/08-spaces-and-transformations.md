## 1. Room on Rails — empty studio furnished in one move

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-1.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
Opening frame: an empty compact studio with warm white walls, oak flooring and one tall window on the left. A low walnut shelving wall stands across the far end. Camera at chest height in the open doorway, wide architectural view with straight vertical lines. Afternoon sunlight and the window shadow remain fixed throughout. No people.

0–2s: the middle panel of the shelving wall slides forward along two visible floor tracks. 2–6s: this single moving module reveals a built-in desk on its left side and a folded upholstered bench on its right; the desk pivots down once and the bench seat unfolds. 6–8s: the module stops midway into the room, dividing work and rest areas. 8–10s: camera gently pushes into the finished arrangement, with the original window still visible.

Audio: quiet rollers, two muted hinge clicks and one soft stop. Every furnishing must be physically attached to the original module; no objects materialize. Preserve room proportions, shelf contents and uninterrupted flooring. No cuts, new doors, shifting window, wall growth, people, brand names or captions.
```

## 2. Courtyard Fold — a miniature garden unfolds

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-2.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
A closed square terracotta planter sits on a pale workbench, seen from a steep three-quarter angle. Its four thick hinged sides meet around a single small olive tree planted in a central soil pocket. Tiny stone paths and moss strips are visibly packed upright against the inside panels. Warm morning light; a blurred greenhouse behind. The whole object fits inside the portrait frame with room around its base.

0–2s: four small latches release in sequence. 2–7s: all four side panels lower slowly outward on their bottom hinges, exposing the paths and moss as they become a flat miniature courtyard around the central tree. 7–10s: the camera lowers slightly and makes a small clockwise arc, revealing the completed garden from path level. The olive tree sways once and settles.

Audio: four ceramic latch ticks, soft hinge friction and greenhouse birds far away. Treat the planter as a designed folding model, not fast plant growth. All garden elements exist from the beginning and rotate with their panels. Keep tree size, soil volume and terracotta color fixed. No extra trees, spilling soil, text or music.
```

## 3. Quiet Section — a house model opens to reveal rooms

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-3.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A precise unbranded architectural model of a two-story timber house stands on a gray display plinth. Front three-quarter view, neutral studio backdrop, soft side lighting. The roof and the front facade form one visibly separate shell with a fine vertical seam at the right corner. Through one small side window, a staircase and a warm interior lamp can already be seen. No human figures.

0–2s: the shell loosens and begins moving straight left on a concealed horizontal support. 2–6s: it slides one house-width away, exposing two furnished floors and a continuous stair inside the stationary rear structure. 6–10s: the shell stops; camera makes a slow lateral move right to show the depth of the open rooms and the stair connection, ending on a clear cutaway.

Audio: subdued sliding mechanism, a single wooden stop and quiet studio air. Preserve every floor height, wall thickness and window position. Interior furniture remains fixed to the floors; roof stays attached to the moving facade. No dissolving walls, new floors, changing room count, miniature people, labels, blueprints or camera cuts.
```

## 4. Shelf to Street — a bookend becomes a miniature alley

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-4.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
Opening frame: a narrow handmade wooden book nook sits between two plain clothbound books on a real shelf. Inside the nook is a miniature cobbled alley, two tiny shop windows and a distant arch, all unlettered. A desk lamp casts warm light from outside. Camera starts far enough back to show both neighboring books and the wooden frame, establishing the model's actual small size.

0–3s: the camera pushes slowly toward the alley entrance. 3–7s: pass through the wooden frame in one continuous move, bringing chipped cobbles and miniature window glass into close view. 7–10s: stop before the distant arch; a tiny loose paper scrap turns once in a light draft while one window lamp gently brightens. The street remains visibly handcrafted, with subtle paint and glue texture.

Audio: soft room ambience gives way to a small airy draft and a faint paper scrape. No voices or soundtrack. This is a camera-scale reveal, not a magical enlargement: keep buildings, arch and shop arrangement identical. No people appearing, real full-size city replacement, moving books, readable signs, logos or cuts.
```

## 5. Reading Orbit — a circular library wall rotates

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-5.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A small circular reading room has a curved oak bookshelf around its rear half and a fixed cream armchair in the center. An arc-shaped opening on the far left exposes a plain cream wall. A fixed writing alcove lies directly behind the chair, initially concealed by the rear shelving. Blank-spined books sit upright in shallow recesses. Wide eye-level view from the doorway; a round ceiling light gives even warm illumination, with no daylight changes.

0–2s: a narrow floor track beneath the shelf becomes apparent as the curved shelving starts rotating clockwise around the stationary chair. 2–7s: the entire rear shelf moves as one rigid piece through a quarter-turn, carrying its existing opening from left toward center. 7–10s: the opening aligns directly behind the chair, framing the writing alcove, and the shelf stops. Camera stays locked to make the transformation readable.

Audio: low smooth motor, gentle wood resonance, one quiet stop. Keep chair, ceiling, alcove and room shell fixed. Books remain seated in the moving shelves; no falls or changing titles. Preserve circular geometry and object spacing. No hidden cuts, duplicating furniture, new windows, captions or music.
```

## 6. Paper Canopy — a folded pavilion rises from a table

[TXT](text/en-US/08-spaces-and-transformations-6.txt) · [↑ Index](#index)

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 5s · 16:9 · 720p

```text
A long accordion-folded sheet of thick ivory paper lies almost flat on a dark architect's worktable. Its two ends are attached to small wooden sliding blocks on straight parallel tracks. Every crease is visible in low raking light. View at tabletop height from one end, with a plain studio background and no drawing tools. The paper occupies the center of the frame with clear space above it.

0–1s: the two wooden blocks begin moving slowly toward each other. 1–4s: the sheet rises along its existing folds into a continuous arched canopy, forming a miniature walk-through pavilion with an open central tunnel. 4–5s: blocks stop; camera advances a few centimeters toward the tunnel entrance as the paper makes one small elastic settling movement.

Audio: dry paper flex, low wooden sliding friction and a final soft crease crackle. The entire canopy comes from the original single sheet; retain its ivory color, thickness and fold count. No new panels, glue, hands, growth, stone material transformation, architecture outside the tabletop, music, labels or edits.
```
