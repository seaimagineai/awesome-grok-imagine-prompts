# Grok Imagine 1.5 Prompts for Ads & Product Videos

[← Back to the main collection](../README.md) · [Next: Cinematic storytelling →](02-cinematic-storytelling.md)

Six original commercial prompts designed around short, legible product actions. Replace bracketed placeholders with your own product details. If a logo or label must remain exact, supply it as a reference image and make it a continuity lock instead of asking the model to invent it.

## 1. Dew Drop Laboratory — skincare serum macro

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 8s · 16:9 · 1080p

```text
Opening frame: a completely original, unbranded frosted-glass serum bottle stands on a wet
slab of pale green stone inside a minimal botanical laboratory. Macro product photography,
100mm lens feeling, centered silhouette, soft daylight through leaves, clean sage and ivory
palette. One transparent serum droplet hangs from the pipette above the open bottle.

Action: the droplet falls in real time, strikes the liquid surface, and produces one controlled
crown splash. A low ring of mist rolls behind the bottle while three water beads travel slowly
down the frosted glass. Camera performs a six-centimeter push-in with stable focus; bottle,
pipette, and pedestal never move.

Audio: close liquid drop, gentle glass resonance, faint greenhouse air. No voice or music.

Continuity: keep bottle geometry, liquid color, pipette alignment, stone texture, and light
direction fixed. No label, logo, flowers appearing, duplicate bottle, floating glass, giant
splash, geometry drift, unreadable text, or watermark. End on a clean catalog-ready hero frame.
```

## 2. Cold Brew Eclipse — coffee launch film

**Mode:** image-to-video · **Output:** 9s · 1:1 · 1080p

```text
Preserve the supplied cold-brew bottle, exact label, cap, coffee color, circular black stone
platform, and amber rim light. The product remains upright and rigid at center frame.

0–3s: camera begins a slow clockwise quarter-orbit. A thin ribbon of cream enters from the top
right and curls behind the bottle, never covering the label. Condensation beads glint naturally.
0–7s: roasted coffee beans roll in a shallow ring around the pedestal with believable friction;
one bean stops against the base. The cream ribbon disperses into a soft marbled cloud in the
background, not inside the sealed bottle.
7–9s: orbit settles at a three-quarter angle. A narrow highlight passes once across the label
and the background motion becomes still.

Audio: three dry bean taps, quiet liquid swirl, low refined bass pulse that fades before the
final second. No speech.

Continuity: exact product silhouette, label spelling, cap, liquid level, scale, and shadows.
No bottle rotation independent of the platform, no label mutation, extra ingredients, foam
explosion, logos added by the model, or camera cut.
```

## 3. Street-to-Studio — performance shoe demonstration

**Mode:** reference-to-video · **References:** `<IMAGE_0>` athlete, `<IMAGE_1>` shoe, `<IMAGE_2>` set · **Output:** 10s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
Use the adult athlete's identity, face, body proportions, and hairstyle from <IMAGE_0>. They
wear the exact original, unbranded performance shoes from <IMAGE_1> in the clean graphite
testing studio from <IMAGE_2>. Plain charcoal training clothes; no additional accessories.

0–3s: low camera follows one natural walking step onto a matte force plate. The shoe compresses

music, crowd, or text.

Continuity: preserve identity, outfit, shoe panel geometry, sole thickness, colors, and both
```

## 4. Doorstep Dinner — food delivery social ad

**Mode:** text-to-video · **Output:** 10s · 9:16 · 1080p

```text
Opening frame: warm evening apartment entryway, realistic vertical social ad. A fictional adult
resident opens the door as an unbranded kraft-paper meal bag rests on a small wooden stool. The
bag is sealed, clean, and centered in the lower third; cozy kitchen light beyond the doorway,
cool blue corridor behind camera, natural skin and lived-in home details.

0–3s: gentle handheld push-in as the resident notices the bag and smiles. 3–7s: match cut by
natural hand occlusion to a top-down kitchen table: the same hands unfold the bag and reveal a
colorful grain bowl, soup cup, and bread arranged securely, with modest steam. 7–10s: close-up
as a spoon breaks the surface of the soup; camera racks focus to the full meal and settles.

Audio: door latch, paper fold, ceramic contact, soft home ambience. One natural spoken line:
“Dinner solved.” No jingle or subtitles.

Continuity: same bag, hands, sleeves, meal components, warm palette, and realistic food scale.
No delivery-company marks, extra dishes, food morphing, messy spill, exaggerated steam,
unreadable packaging text, or glamour retouching.
```

## 5. Silver Current — artisan jewelry reveal

**Mode:** image-to-video · **Output:** 7s · 3:2 · 1080p

```text
Preserve the supplied handmade silver pendant, chain links, stone setting, brushed scratches,
dark slate surface, and narrow window reflection. This is a quiet artisan object study.

Camera makes a continuous fifteen-degree macro orbit from left to right. A soft strip of daylight
travels across the brushed silver, revealing real micro-scratches without polishing them away.
The chain settles by two millimeters as if tension was gently released; every link remains
connected and identical. The small set stone catches one restrained highlight, not a glow.
Fine dust in the background drifts through shallow focus while the pendant stays sharp.

Audio: faint chain contact on slate, quiet room tone, one distant workshop tap. No music,
voice, or added text.

Continuity: exact pendant silhouette, stone shape and color, link count near the pendant,
surface wear, scale, and shadow. No gem enlargement, duplicated chain, liquid metal, magical
particles, extra jewelry, invented engraving, logo, hands, or jump cut. Finish completely still.
```

## 6. One Tap Away — clean mobile app promo

**Mode:** reference-to-video · **References:** `<IMAGE_0>` phone UI, `<IMAGE_1>` hand model · **Output:** 8s · 9:16 · 720p

```text
Use the exact fictional app interface and phone screen from <IMAGE_0>. Use only the hand shape,
skin tone, and plain sleeve from <IMAGE_1>. Place the phone above a light oak desk with a paper
notebook and one ceramic pen cup, soft morning daylight, realistic product-demo photography.

0–3s: overhead camera descends slowly as the hand enters and taps the single primary button.

Audio: fingertip tap, subtle interface confirmation tone, phone contact on wood, quiet room.

Continuity: exact screen layout and text, phone proportions, hand anatomy, sleeve, desk objects,

brand marks, pop-up text, or dramatic holograms.
```

## Commercial prompt checklist

- Use a reference image whenever product geometry or label accuracy is important.
- Keep the hero object rigid; let light, condensation, particles, fabric, or camera provide motion.
- Give every ingredient a physical path and an ending state.
- Reserve at least one second of stillness for the final product frame.
- Ask for voice, music, and Foley separately so the mix has a clear priority.
